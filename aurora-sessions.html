Important to know

The Aurora is a natural phenomenon.

Its strength, visibility, and colours can never be guaranteed. Your booking is for a private photography session — not a guaranteed Aurora appearance.

Cloud cover, weather, or safety conditions can make a session unsuitable. If that happens, we will offer to reschedule during your stay or for the next suitable date, subject to availability.
